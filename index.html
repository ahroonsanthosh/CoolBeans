Our Story

A Passion for Coffee,
Born in Cork

Tucked into the heart of Cork City on Parnell Place, Cool Beans Coffee is more than a café — it's a destination for those who believe great coffee changes the tone of a day.

We source, roast, and brew with obsessive care. From our signature espresso blends to single-origin pour-overs, every cup is a quiet act of craft. Our dark, warmly lit space — with its chalkboard walls and Edison glow — is a place to slow down, connect, and savour.
